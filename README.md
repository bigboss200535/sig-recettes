## Sig-reccetes
Sig-reccetes is a lightweight web application designed to help organizations manage tax payments and maintain a structured registry of vendors. It provides an organized workflow for recording transactions, tracking payment statuses, and storing vendor information in a centralized and easy-to-use interface.

Features
- Vendor Management
Add, edit, and organize vendor profiles with key business details.

- Vendor Lookup
Add, edit, and view vendors on live maps with their co-ordinates

- Tax Payment Tracking
Record payments, update their status, and maintain a clear historical log.

- Fines or penalty payment

- Outdoor billboard and advertisement payments

- Search & Filtering
Quickly locate vendors or payment records through built-in search tools.

- Role-Based Access
Manage permissions for developers, administrators, auditors, or staff users, revenue officer and municipal heads.


## Usage
- Navigate to the dashboard to view vendor and tax summaries.
- Use the vendor module to add or update supplier information.
- Record tax payments, update their statuses, and review historical logs.
